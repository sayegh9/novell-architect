### Chapter 1 (1839 words)
**Summary:** 

**Opening:** The noise was a fist of light punching through his skull. Orange bursts from the smithy next door, the percussive clang of a sonic hammer shaping steel. A saw-toothed line of angry red, the screech of a new apprentice mishandling a lathe two benches down. Everywhere, the air was thick with the grit of sound, a storm of jagged color that left a taste like rust and hot metal on his tongue. Cassian hunched over the workbench, trying to shrink the world to the small bronze bell in his left hand. He pressed the heels of his palms to his temples, a useless gesture. The sound came through the bone. He wore his thickest wool tunic, the collar high and tight against his neck, but the scratch of the fabric was just one more texture in the chaos. He needed a clean line. One pure, unwavering thread of light to...

**Closing:** ...chaotic colors of the workshop seemed to bend away from it, leaving a small pocket of quiet darkness. It did not absorb sound; it seemed to negate it. A void. He swept the last of the dust and metal filings away from its heavy threshold. He leaned on the broom, the rough wood a solid presence in his hands. He looked at the intricate brass lock, a puzzle he was not allowed to solve. Behind it was the only true silence in the House of Bells, and he had no idea what it was keeping in, or what it was keeping out. A flicker of something, a thought like a pale and unfamiliar color, moved through him. A question about the nature of cages. He shook his head, pushing it away. It was a dangerous thought. A useless one. He had a floor to finish, and a father to obey.
