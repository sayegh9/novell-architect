# Working Title: The Second Son of the House of Bells

HOOK: In a city where tonal law binds reality, a bellmaker's second son discovers his father's forbidden harmonic.

WORLD: Cantamura, built inside a natural amphitheater where pitch governance dictates civic hierarchy.

MAGIC/COST: Tonal Law enables physical binding, but exposure causes irreversible acoustic tremor.

CENTRAL LIE: If I master the institutional system, I can protect my family from the inside.

CORE TENSION: Revealing the forbidden harmonic can free his brother from the Corda contract, but will shatter the tonal resonance holding the city's foundations together.
