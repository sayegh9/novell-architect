### Chapter 1 (1009 words)
**Summary:** 

**Opening:** The noise was a fist of light punching through his skull. Orange bursts from the smithy next door, the percussive clang of a sonic hammer shaping steel. A saw-toothed line of angry red, the screech of a new apprentice mishandling a lathe two benches down. The air was thick with the grit of sound, a storm of jagged color that left a taste like rust on his tongue. Cassian hunched over the workbench, trying to shrink the world to the small bronze bell in his left hand. He tapped its tines with a felt-headed mallet. The sound that rose was a warm, liquid bronze, the color of the metal itself. But around its edges shimmered a thin, acidic halo of yellow-green. Dissonance. A fraction of a cent too high, a sharpness that felt like a needle sliding in just behind his eye. His jaw tightened. He drew a fine-toothed file...

**Closing:** ...black iron. There was no handle, no latch, only a complex lock of interlocking brass rings. He had been forbidden to touch it for his entire life. He had swept this floor a thousand times, but today he stopped. He looked at the door, at the absolute stillness of the air around it. The chaotic colors of the workshop seemed to bend away from it, leaving a small pocket of quiet darkness. It did not absorb sound; it negated it. A void. He swept the last of the dust away from its heavy threshold. He leaned on the broom, the rough wood a solid presence in his hands. He looked at the intricate brass lock, a puzzle he was not allowed to solve. Behind it was the only true silence in the House of Bells. He had no idea what it was keeping in, or what it was keeping out.
