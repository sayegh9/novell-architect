This is CHARACTERS.MD. It is the definitive reference for all persons of interest in *The Second Son of the House of Bells*. This document is canon.

***

### 1. Cassian “Cass” Bellwright

*   **Age:** 19
*   **Role:** Protagonist, POV Character. A second son and apprentice bellmaker in the House of Bells.

*   **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
    *   **Ghost:** At age fourteen, witnessing his brilliant older brother, Perin, be fitted with a Corda contract ring. He saw the light drain from Perin’s eyes as the binding chord settled, and heard his father’s choked silence, which felt like complicity.
    *   **Wound:** A deep-seated feeling of powerlessness and a belief that he is inadequate to protect the people he loves.
    *   **Lie:** *If I master the institutional system, I can protect my family from the inside.* He believes that by becoming a perfect Resonator and gaining influence within the Guild, he can earn the power to negotiate Perin’s freedom.
    *   **Want:** To win a coveted apprenticeship at the Aurally Conservatorium, believing it’s the only path to the status required to free his brother.
    *   **Need:** To accept that the system itself is the source of his family’s suffering and that true protection requires breaking it, not mastering it.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 2 -> 8. Starts as a reactor, driven by his father's demands and his brother's fate. His discovery of the Null Chord forces him to become the primary driver of the plot.
    *   **Likability:** 8. He is earnest, loyal, and carries a heavy burden. His sensory struggles make him vulnerable and easy to empathize with.
    *   **Competence:** 4 -> 9. Initially seen as a flawed Resonator with an "unstable" pitch. His true competence lies in his unique synesthetic perception, a soft magic he must learn to control and weaponize. His journey is one of realizing his perceived weakness is his greatest strength.

*   **Arc Type:** Positive. Cass will learn to trust his unique perception, reject the societal definition of perfection, and embrace the terrible power he has discovered, ultimately choosing to break the system to save his brother.

*   **Speech Pattern:**
    1.  **Vocabulary:** Simple, tactile, sensory. Avoids abstract Aurally jargon.
    2.  **Sentence Length:** Often short, clipped sentences when overwhelmed. Longer, more flowing sentences when describing sound as color and texture.
    3.  **Formality:** Informal. Uses contractions. Respectful but not subservient.
    4.  **Verbal Tics:** Often starts sentences with "It sounds like..." or "It looks like..." when trying to explain his synesthesia to others. Tends to trail off when a sound becomes painful.
    5.  **Question Ratio:** High. He is constantly trying to understand the disconnect between what he is told and what he perceives.
    6.  **Interruptions:** Rarely interrupts. He is an observer, a listener by nature.
    7.  **Metaphor Domain:** Exclusively sensory. Sound is light, texture, taste, or pain. "Her voice was the color of rust." "The chord felt like smooth, cold glass."
    8.  **Directness:** Indirect. He hides his perceptions and true thoughts for fear of being seen as broken or heretical.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "It’s not off-key. It’s... empty. It’s the shape of the space where the note is supposed to be."
        *   "When you sing the Bind, it looks like a braid of solid gold light. But father’s harmonic... it’s different. It’s like a thread of smoke that unweaves the braid."
        *   "Can’t you hear that? Underneath the workshop noise? It’s a jagged green line. It hurts my teeth."

*   **Physical Appearance:** Tall and lean, with the wiry muscle of a craftsman. His hands are calloused but his fingers are long and sensitive. Dark, unruly hair that he constantly pushes out of his eyes. His eyes are a pale grey, often squinted against the sensory assault of the city. He has a slight, persistent frown of concentration.

*   **Physical Habits:**
    *   Pressing the heels of his hands against his eyes or temples to block out overwhelming sensory input.
    *   Tracing the shape of sounds he sees in the air with a finger.
    *   Wears a thick, high-collared wool tunic even in warm weather, as the texture helps ground him.
    *   Constantly tapping his fingers on his leg, silently practicing intervals.

*   **Secrets:**
    1.  He has discovered the Null Chord, a harmonic that can unravel Tonal Law, and is hiding this knowledge from everyone.
    2.  A part of him was relieved when Perin, the perfect older brother he could never live up to, was taken away. The guilt from this thought haunts him.

*   **Key Relationships:**
    *   **Eddan (Father):** A relationship of friction and frustrated love. Cass seeks his father's approval but constantly clashes with his rigid adherence to tradition.
    *   **Perin (Brother):** The driving force of his life. An idealized figure whose absence defines Cass’s goals.
    *   **Maret Corda:** His direct antagonist. She represents the unyielding logic of the system he seeks to both master and destroy.
    *   **Elara (Peer):** His only confidant, who represents the system's allure and its potential for good, forcing him to question if destruction is the only answer.

*   **Thematic Role:** Embodies the conflict between individual conscience and systemic stability. He asks: *Is a beautiful, orderly world worth building on a foundation of human suffering?*

### 2. Eddan Bellwright

*   **Age:** 52
*   **Role:** Cass's father, Master of the House of Bells.

*   **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
    *   **Ghost:** Watching his own father, a brilliant and experimental bellmaker, succumb to the Great Tremor Plague. He saw his father’s hands shake so violently he could no longer hold a tuning fork, his mind reduced to a single, wavering hum.
    *   **Wound:** A deep-seated terror of deviation and a crippling fear that ambition leads to ruin.
    *   **Lie:** *Absolute adherence to orthodox Tonal Law is the only way to ensure survival.* He believes any experimentation is a step towards the same fate that took his father.
    *   **Want:** To force Cass to abandon his "unstable" sensitivities, become a conventional Resonator, and secure a safe, unremarkable future for the House of Bells.
    *   **Need:** To trust his son’s unique abilities and confront the truth that the system he so fears to offend is already destroying his family.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 3. He is reactive and defensive, defined by what he wants to prevent, not what he wants to achieve. His actions are about enforcing limits on Cass.
    *   **Likability:** 4. His fear makes him rigid and often cruel to Cass, but his motivations are rooted in a desperate, tragic love. The reader understands him but does not root for him.
    *   **Competence:** 9. He is a master craftsman. His bells are flawless. His knowledge of orthodox Tonal Law is second to none in the Guilds.

*   **Arc Type:** Flat / Negative. He is a man trapped by his trauma, unable to change his core beliefs even when faced with evidence that they are wrong. He represents the tragedy of the old guard.

*   **Speech Pattern:**
    1.  **Vocabulary:** Precise, archaic, craft-focused. "Purity," "temper," "resonance," "tolerance."
    2.  **Sentence Length:** Short, declarative statements. Commands and aphorisms.
    3.  **Formality:** High. No contractions. Addresses Cass as "boy" or "Cassian," never "Cass."
    4.  **Verbal Tics:** Frequent use of workshop metaphors for life. "A man, like a bell, is defined by his impurities."
    5.  **Question Ratio:** Very low. He deals in pronouncements, not inquiries.
    6.  **Interruptions:** Interrupts Cass frequently to "correct" him.
    7.  **Metaphor Domain:** Metallurgy, acoustics, tradition. "Tradition is the annealing fire that strengthens the soul."
    8.  **Directness:** Blunt and direct to the point of cruelty.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "You will focus on the Fifth. There is no other path. There is no other work."
        *   "That is not music. It is noise. It is the sound of collapse."
        *   "Do not speak to me of what you *see*. Sound is to be heard, measured, and controlled. Nothing more."

*   **Physical Appearance:** Broad-shouldered but stooped, with a perpetual tension in his neck. His face is lined from soot and worry. Close-cropped grey hair and a neatly trimmed beard. His eyes are the same pale grey as Cass's, but hard and unforgiving.

*   **Physical Habits:**
    *   His left hand trembles constantly, a symptom of early-stage Acoustic Tremor. He often hides it in his pocket or braces it against his workbench.
    *   Wipes his hands on a leather apron, even when they are clean. A nervous, grounding gesture.
    *   Taps objects with a steel tuning fork, listening to their resonant frequency out of habit.

*   **Secrets:**
    1.  His tremor is not from his own work, but from a single, massive backlash he suffered as a young man while attempting to cast a complex binding to save his dying father. It failed.
    2.  Before he died, his father was secretly researching Enharmonic theory—the very heresy Eddan now fears. Eddan found his father's journals filled with forbidden harmonics and burned them, believing he was protecting his family's name.

*   **Key Relationships:**
    *   **Cass (Son):** He loves Cass but sees his unique gift as the same dangerous deviancy that he believes killed his own father. He tries to crush it out of fear.
    *   **Perin (Son):** The ghost of his ambition. He pushed Perin to succeed and blames himself for the boy's downfall, which only reinforces his conservative stance with Cass.

*   **Thematic Role:** Represents tradition as a cage and fear as a form of paralysis. He asks: *What is the cost of safety?*

### 3. Perin Bellwright

*   **Age:** 23
*   **Role:** The Inciting Incident. Cass's older brother, bound by a Corda contract.

*   **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
    *   **Ghost:** Being lauded as a prodigy his whole life, the great hope of the House of Bells, only to find that his talent could not protect him from the rigid mechanics of Guild politics and debt.
    *   **Wound:** The humiliation of his bondage and the bitter knowledge that his ambition led him into a trap.
    *   **Lie:** *Personal excellence and ambition are enough to conquer any system.* He believed his superior skill would allow him to bend the rules to his will.
    *   **Want:** To be free of his Corda contract and restore his family's honor, which he feels he single-handedly destroyed.
    *   **Need:** To protect Cass from making the same mistakes and to understand that the system itself is rigged against individual ambition.

*   **Three Sliders (Pre-Contract):**
    *   **Proactivity:** 9. Ambitious, driven, always taking the lead. He sought out the loan that led to his contract.
    *   **Likability:** 6. Charismatic and charming, but with an arrogant edge.
    *   **Competence:** 10. A true vocal prodigy. He could perform complex harmonics at sixteen that most masters struggled with.

*   **Arc Type:** Static (as a presence). His story has already happened, and he serves as a constant, unchanging motivation for Cass.

*   **Speech Pattern (in flashbacks/letters):**
    1.  **Vocabulary:** Expansive and confident. Uses the formal language of the Aurally with ease.
    2.  **Sentence Length:** Complex and flowing.
    3.  **Formality:** A calculated mix. Formal to superiors, casually confident to peers.
    4.  **Verbal Tics:** A tendency to use musical terms to describe success or failure ("a perfect resolution," "a dissonant outcome").
    5.  **Question Ratio:** Low. He spoke in confident statements.
    6.  **Interruptions:** Would often finish others' sentences, not out of rudeness, but from quickness of mind.
    7.  **Metaphor Domain:** Music and performance. "Politics is just a symphony of competing interests."
    8.  **Directness:** Bold and direct.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "Father worries about the foundation. I intend to build the spire."
        *   "The Corda loan is just an overture, Cass. The real performance is yet to come."

*   **Physical Appearance:** In memory, he is the idealized Bellwright son: tall, strong, with a confident smile and clear, resonant eyes. The present reality (if seen) would be a gaunt, weary version, the light of his ambition extinguished.

*   **Secrets:**
    1.  His Corda contract was not for a simple defaulted loan. He discovered that Rector Suvaine was using forbidden Enharmonic intervals to patch the failing foundations of the Conductor's Spire. He tried to use this information as leverage, and the contract was the Rector's way of silencing him permanently.

*   **Key Relationships:**
    *   **Cass (Brother):** He was both a role model and a rival to Cass. His current state is Cass's primary motivation.
    *   **Rector Suvaine (Antagonist):** He knows her secret, making him a living threat to the most powerful person in the city.

*   **Thematic Role:** The ghost of ambition, the embodiment of the system’s promise and its brutal punishment for failure.

### 4. Maret Corda

*   **Age:** 35
*   **Role:** Antagonist. The Guildmistress of the Corda notary house, responsible for enforcing all Corda contracts.

*   **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
    *   **Ghost:** Her family, minor Guild members, was ruined when a business partner broke a contract secured only by a handshake. They were cast into the Basin, and she watched her parents waste away from shame and poverty.
    *   **Wound:** A pathological fear of social and economic chaos, and a deep-seated contempt for trust and honor, which she sees as weaknesses.
    *   **Lie:** *Order, enforced by unbreakable contracts, is the only thing preventing society from collapsing into chaos.*
    *   **Want:** To expand the power and reach of the Corda contract system until every significant transaction in Cantamura is bound by its inescapable, perfect order.
    *   **Need:** To learn that true strength lies in mercy and trust, and that a system without compassion is just a more elegant form of tyranny.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 9. She is a relentless force, actively seeking to expand her influence and enforce her worldview.
    *   **Likability:** 3 -> 6. Initially appears as a cold, unfeeling bureaucrat. As her backstory is revealed, her motivations become understandable, if not agreeable.
    *   **Competence:** 9. She is a master of Tonal Law, specializing in the complex chord progressions of the Corda. She is also a brilliant politician and strategist.

*   **Arc Type:** Flat / Mirror. She serves as a dark mirror to Cass. While he seeks to unravel a binding, she seeks to create more. She will not change, forcing Cass to defeat her ideology, not just her person.

*   **Speech Pattern:**
    1.  **Vocabulary:** Legalistic, precise, formal. "Obligation," "consequence," "stipulation," "default."
    2.  **Sentence Length:** Medium to long, perfectly structured. Each sentence is a closed system.
    3.  **Formality:** Extremely high. No contractions, ever.
    4.  **Verbal Tics:** A habit of restating others' emotional arguments in cold, logical terms.
    5.  **Question Ratio:** Very low. Asks questions only to confirm facts, never to explore possibilities.
    6.  **Interruptions:** Never interrupts. She waits patiently for the other person to finish, then calmly dismantles their argument.
    7.  **Metaphor Domain:** Law, architecture, mechanics. "Emotion is a crack in the foundation of reason." "A contract is a perfectly balanced structure."
    8.  **Directness:** Utterly and unnervingly direct. She never uses subtext.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "Your brother’s intent is irrelevant. The terms of the default are absolute."
        *   "Compassion is a variable. The Law must be a constant."
        *   "You speak of freedom. I speak of the consequences of its abuse. We are not discussing the same matter."

*   **Physical Appearance:** Severe and immaculate. Hair pulled back in a tight, geometric bun with not a single stray strand. Wears a dark, perfectly tailored robe of office. Her face is sharp angles and pale skin. Her eyes are dark and watchful.

*   **Physical Habits:**
    *   Compulsively straightens objects on her desk—inkwells, scrolls, weights—into perfect alignment.
    *   Her hands are perfectly still when she speaks. No fidgeting, no wasted motion.
    *   When casting a Corda, her tremor (from backlash) is in her right hand, but she has trained herself to hold it so still it is almost imperceptible.

*   **Secrets:**
    1.  She holds a secret Corda contract on her own ailing mother, binding her to stay within a sanatorium that provides the best care. She tells herself it is to ensure her mother follows doctor's orders, but it is truly born of her terror of losing the last of her family. It is a gross misuse of her power, born of love and fear.

*   **Key Relationships:**
    *   **Cass (Adversary):** She sees his desire to break Perin's contract not as an act of love, but as a dangerous anarchist impulse that threatens the entire city's stability.
    *   **Rector Suvaine (Ally):** A tense alliance. Maret provides the Rector with the tools of control, and the Rector provides the institutional legitimacy Maret craves.

*   **Thematic Role:** The embodiment of order without justice. She asks: *Can a system be perfect if it has no room for mercy?*

### 5. Rector Suvaine

*   **Age:** 68
*   **Role:** Institutional Antagonist. Chancellor of the Aurally Conservatorium and de facto leader of Cantamura.

*   **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
    *   **Ghost:** As a young acolyte, she studied the historical records of the Schism of the Fifth, seeing firsthand the city-shattering destruction wrought by tonal heresy.
    *   **Wound:** A bone-deep conviction that doctrinal purity is the only shield against annihilation.
    *   **Lie:** *The Prime Chord and its Orthodox interpretation are sacred and perfect. Any deviation, for any reason, is a corruption that will lead to the city's destruction.*
    *   **Want:** To maintain the stability of Cantamura and the absolute authority of the Aurally by suppressing all doctrinal threats and heretical research.
    *   **Need:** To acknowledge that the foundations she protects are already crumbling and that her rigid refusal to adapt is the true threat to the city.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 8. She is the system's immune response, actively seeking out and neutralizing threats to the status quo.
    *   **Likability:** 2. She is cold, condescending, and utterly convinced of her own righteousness. She sacrifices people for abstract principles.
    *   **Competence:** 10. A master of Tonal Law on a theoretical and practical level unmatched in the city. A ruthless and effective political operator.

*   **Speech Pattern:**
    1.  **Vocabulary:** Academic, abstract, Latinate. "Ontological," "consonance," "doctrinal integrity."
    2.  **Sentence Length:** Long, complex, often employing the passive voice to distance herself from her actions.
    3.  **Formality:** The highest possible.
    4.  **Verbal Tics:** Using "we" to mean the Aurally Council, even when stating her own opinion.
    5.  **Question Ratio:** Zero. She does not ask; she informs.
    6.  **Interruptions:** Never. She considers it beneath her.
    7.  **Metaphor Domain:** Theology, history, music theory.
    8.  **Directness:** Deceptively indirect. She couches threats in academic discourse and ultimatums in polite suggestions.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "It has been determined that your brother's continued civic utility outweighs his personal discomfort."
        *   "The integrity of the Prime Chord is a matter of civic survival, not a subject for Guild-level debate."
        *   "One must not allow sentiment to introduce a dissonance into the harmony of the state."

*   **Secrets:**
    1.  The Prime Chord is failing. The city's foundations are suffering from Resonant Decay far faster than predicted. She is secretly using forbidden Enharmonic principles—the very heresy she publicly condemns—in a desperate, failing attempt to patch the cracks without causing a panic. Perin Bellwright discovered this, which is why he had to be silenced.

*   **Thematic Role:** The tyranny of the greater good. She asks: *How many people must be sacrificed to save a beautiful idea?*

### 6. Elara of the House of Glass

*   **Age:** 19
*   **Role:** Peer and Confidant. A gifted student at the Conservatorium and Cass's only friend.

*   **Ghost:** She was born with perfect pitch and a pure voice, and has known nothing but praise and success her entire life. She has never experienced the system's cruelty.
*   **Lie:** *The system is just and rewards true talent. Any failure is the fault of the individual's impurity or lack of skill.*
*   **Want:** To become a High Conductor and serve the city by maintaining the purity of the Prime Chord.
*   **Need:** To see the suffering the system inflicts on those less fortunate and to question the morality of the institution she serves.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 5. She acts within the system, but is willing to bend rules to help Cass, driving key subplots.
    *   **Likability:** 9. She is kind, brilliant, and fiercely loyal to Cass, even when she doesn't understand him.
    *   **Competence:** 9. A vocal prodigy, second only to what Perin was. She understands orthodox theory better than Cass does.

*   **Speech Pattern:** Earnest, technical, and precise. She speaks of music theory with the passion others reserve for poetry. She is direct and guileless.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "But the math doesn't work, Cass. A null harmonic would violate the First Principle of Acoustic Conservation. It's impossible."
        *   "I heard your overtone series during the exam. It was... perfect. Perfectly wrong. How did you do that?"

*   **Thematic Role:** The good person working for a corrupt system. She represents the system's seductive promise and forces Cass to consider what will be lost if he tears it all down.

### 7. Kaelen, the Tone-Deaf

*   **Age:** 28
*   **Role:** The Revolutionary. A high-ranking operative in the Silenti.

*   **Ghost:** Tested at age five and declared "tonally inert," a source of shame for her Resonator family, who abandoned her to the Basin.
*   **Lie:** *The only way to achieve justice is to shatter the system and all who benefit from it. There is no middle ground.*
*   **Want:** To find the Null Chord—what the Silenti call the "Sound of Liberation"—and use it to bring down the Aurally Council and the Guilds.
*   **Need:** To learn the difference between liberation and destruction.

*   **Three Sliders:**
    *   **Proactivity:** 8. She actively seeks to recruit Cass and seize his discovery for her own cause.
    *   **Likability:** 5. She is cynical, ruthless, and pragmatic, but her anger is righteous and born of genuine injustice.
    *   **Competence:** 8. An expert in espionage, infiltration, and the black market. She knows the city's underbelly better than anyone.

*   **Speech Pattern:** Clipped, cynical, and direct. Sentences are short and stripped of ornament. Her metaphors are drawn from the Basin: filth, foundations, and pressure.
    *   **Example Lines:**
        *   "They call us tone-deaf. Good. Means we don't have to listen to their pretty lies."
        *   "Your brother is a symptom. The Aurally are the disease. You don't cure a plague by negotiating with one of the sick."
        *   "Don't talk to me about saving the city. This city is a cage. You don't polish a cage. You break the lock."

*   **Thematic Role:** The temptation of righteous violence. She represents the anger of the oppressed and asks: *Is total destruction a valid response to total oppression?*
