# Working Title: The Solvency of Saints

HOOK: A self-anointed family patriarch and benevolent financial mentor to his friends conceals a catastrophic market gambling debt, until public collapse strips him of his savior complex and forces him into a six-month locked rehabilitation ward alongside the very outcasts he once looked down upon.

WORLD: Modern metropolis oscillating between high-leverage 4:00 AM crypto trading terminals/VIP patron lounges, a decaying ancestral family apartment weighed down by an elderly analog father and a sacrifice-silenced mother, and "The Equalizer"—a locked, cross-class rehabilitation ward where street dealers, disgraced quants, and surgeons sit on identical plastic chairs.

THEMATIC ENGINE / COST: The Leverage of Playing God. Taking on the financial and emotional survival of others manufactures an intoxicating savior ego and social entitlement. The cost of maintaining the illusion is compulsive high-stakes market gambling, total debt ruin, and the public puncture of identity.

CENTRAL LIE: "If I carry every burden and make myself indispensable to everyone, I am immune to ruin and exempt from ordinary human weakness."

CORE TENSION: Confessing the debt collapse will protect his family from criminal liability and halt the spiral, but doing so destroys the idolized patriarch image his sisters revere, breaks his elderly father's fragile heart, and exposes his entire life as a performance of fraudulent benevolence.

THEMATIC POLARITY: Omnipotent Patronage & Entitlement vs. Radical Vulnerability & Shared Humility.
