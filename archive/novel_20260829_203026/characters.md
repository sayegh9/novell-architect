# CHARACTERS REGISTRY: THE SOLVENCY OF SAINTS

***

### 1. Adam Mansour (Protagonist & POV)

* **Age:** 26
* **Role:** Protagonist, POV Character. The self-made "Precocious Patriarch," financial patron, market speculator, and patient in Ward 4.

* **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
  * **Ghost:** At age twelve, witnessing his elderly, exhausted father break down weeping over an unpaid electrical cut-off notice while neighbors watched. That night, Adam swore he would never let anyone in his house feel helpless again.
  * **Wound:** A chronic, terrorizing fear of irrelevance, poverty, and ordinary human vulnerability.
  * **Lie:** *"If I carry every financial and emotional burden, I become untouchable, indispensable, and exempt from moral collapse."*
  * **Want:** To pull off a multi-million-dollar high-leverage market recovery, pay off all secret shadow debts, restore his family's wealth, and maintain his reputation as a benevolent mastermind.
  * **Need:** To surrender the savior complex, accept his own brokenness and fallibility, sit on the plastic chair as an equal, and allow himself to be loved without buying people's allegiance.

* **Three Sliders:**
  * **Proactivity:** 9 -> 4 -> 7. Hyper-proactive to the point of mania; forces decisions, manipulates markets, controls conversations. In early rehab, forced into total paralysis/submission, before developing healthy, honest agency.
  * **Likability:** 6 -> 3 -> 8. Charismatic, fiercely loyal to his family, and protective, but his arrogance, manipulative patronage, and condescension repel people until his vulnerability is cracked open in rehab.
  * **Competence:** 8 -> 2 -> 6. Brilliant with mathematical patterns, social dynamics, and short-term strategy, but totally blind to his emotional pathology and risk tolerance.

* **Arc Type:** Positive Disillusionment / Redemption Arc.

* **Speech Pattern (8 Dimensions):**
  1. **Vocabulary:** Sharp, analytical, financial-technical mixed with rapid colloquial street cadence.
  2. **Sentence Length:** Fast, staccato, dominant when running schemes; fractured, quiet, monosyllabic when experiencing withdrawal and exposure.
  3. **Formality:** Shifting. Ultra-smooth and authoritative with peers/creditors; tender and carefully guarded with parents; aggressive and defensive when confronted.
  4. **Verbal Tics:** Taps his watch; says "Look at the numbers" or "I've already solved it" to shut down dissent.
  5. **Question/Statement Ratio:** Heavy on statements and rhetorical questions. Rarely asks genuine questions because he assumes he knows the answer.
  6. **Interruptions:** High frequency in early chapters; cuts people off to control the narrative.
  7. **Metaphor Domain:** Liquidity, margin calls, systemic leverage, debts, architectural load-bearing.
  8. **Directness:** Deceptively direct. Gives the appearance of brutal honesty while concealing the actual ledger.
  * **Example Lines:**
    * "You think I do this for the money? The money is just the grease. I do it so nobody in this family ever has to look at the floor when a landlord walks in."
    * "Give me forty-eight hours on the 50x perpetuals. If the funding rate resets, the whole tranche clears."
    * "You want me to talk about my feelings in a circle of junkies? My feelings cost four hundred thousand dollars in unhedged margin."

* **Physical Appearance:** Lean, sharp-featured, dark circles permanently bruised beneath his eyes from three years of sleeping in two-hour increments. Wears tailored but rumpled linen shirts and expensive sneakers that show wear around the soles. A nervous twitch in his left eyelid when leverage positions turn red.

* **Habits & Tells:**
  * Constantly refreshing his phone screen under the edge of dining tables.
  * Checking the door locks three times before sitting down.
  * Rubbing his thumb across his index finger as if counting invisible paper bills.

* **Secrets:**
  1. He liquidated his younger sister Layla's university tuition trust fund three weeks ago to cover an emergency margin call.
  2. He secretly borrowed $60,000 from an underworld shadow creditor using his father's house title deed as forged collateral.

* **Thematic Role:** The Myth of the Autonomous Savior. Asks: *When does love curdle into tyrannical patronage?*

### 2. Yousef Mansour (The Father)

* **Age:** 76 (50-year gap with Adam)
* **Role:** Adam’s Father. Retired analog draftsman and clerk.

* **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
  * **Ghost:** Survived wars, nationalizations, and currency devaluations where hard manual work was rendered worthless overnight by corrupt governments.
  * **Wound:** The humiliation of physical decline, economic obsolescence, and being dependent on a son who treats him like a fragile museum relic.
  * **Lie:** *"Dignity lies in silence, endurance, and pretending the modern world does not exist."*
  * **Want:** To live his remaining days in quiet respectability without being a burden to his children.
  * **Need:** To step into his paternal authority, confront his son’s destructive hubris, and offer unconditional paternal love that isn't contingent on wealth.

* **Three Sliders:** Proactivity: 2 | Likability: 9 | Competence: 6 (master of analog precision, helpless in digital modernity).
* **Arc Type:** Flat / Tragic Pillar.
* **Speech Pattern:** Slow, formal, classical Arabic cadence mixed with measured French loanwords. Speaks in deliberate, unhurried sentences. Never raises his voice; when angry, his voice drops to a dry whisper.
  * *Example Line:* "A man who spends money he has not touched with his hands is not wealthy, Adam. He is merely holding another man’s fire."
* **Habits:** Polishing his steel drafting pens with a chamois cloth; cleaning his spectacles with the corner of his vest; checking his pocket watch against the mosque call to prayer.

***

### 3. Mariam Mansour (The Mother)

* **Age:** 50 (26-year gap with husband, 24-year gap with Adam)
* **Role:** Adam’s Mother. The domestic engine of the household.

* **Ghost/Wound/Need/Lie Chain:**
  * **Ghost:** Married off at age nineteen to a man twenty-six years her senior to secure family stability, abandoning her acceptance to the state teacher's college.
  * **Wound:** An entire adult life spent playing nursemaid to an aging husband and domestic servant to her children, harboring an ocean of smothered talent.
  * **Lie:** *"My sacrifice is only sanctified if my son becomes the undisputed master of the world."*
  * **Want:** For Adam to succeed at all costs, validating every compromise of her lost youth.
  * **Need:** To reclaim her own emotional identity separate from Adam's fortunes and stop acting as the enabler of his delusions.

* **Three Sliders:** Proactivity: 6 | Likability: 8 | Competence: 8 (tactical master of family politics and emotional economy).
* **Speech Pattern:** Rapid, maternal, emotionally saturated. Speaks through food, health anxieties, and indirect guilt.
  * *Example Line:* "Eat the meat, Adam. You look like a ghost. If you fall sick, the whole house collapses into the street."
* **Habits:** Constantly wiping down clean kitchen countertops; counting prayer beads while watching television with the sound muted; smoothing Adam’s collars with trembling fingers.

***

### 4. Nour (22) & Layla (19) Mansour (The Sisters)

* **Roles:** Adam's younger sisters. Nour is a stressed state-university medical intern; Layla is an ambitious graphic design student.
* **The Dynamic:**
  * **Nour:** Astute, observant, carries the unvoiced bitterness of knowing she had to work three jobs because all family funds went to Adam’s early ventures, yet loves him fiercely.
  * **Layla:** Hero-worships Adam. Wears the sneakers he bought her like sacred relics; believes he is an untouchable titan.
* **Thematic Function:** The innocent collateral of Adam's hubris. When Adam’s debt collapses, their futures are the real casualties.

***

### 5. Karim & Bassam (The Patronage Orbit)

* **Roles:** Adam's childhood friends whom he turned into financial dependents and employees.
* **Karim:** The sycophant who strokes Adam's ego to get his nightclub tabs paid and startup ideas funded.
* **Bassam:** The conflicted moral conscience who sees Adam spiraling but stays silent because he owes Adam $15,000 for his mother’s chemotherapy.
* **Thematic Function:** The chorus of Adam’s god-complex. When Adam goes down, Karim is the first to leak the news and sneer, while Bassam is crushed by the debt.

***

### 6. The Equalizer Ward 4 Cohort (Rehab Crucible)

* **Dr. Farouk (58):** Former Chief of Pediatric Surgery, addicted to fentanyl. Sophisticated, arrogant, uses psychiatric vocabulary to deflect from his own catastrophic ruin. Acts as Adam’s early intellectual sparring partner until both are stripped bare.
* **Ziad "Spike" (29):** Corner heroin dealer and former street enforcer from the slums. No education, razor-sharp emotional intuition. He is the first person in Adam's life who refuses to be impressed by numbers or money. He calls out Adam's savior complex in open group therapy.
  * *Example Line:* "You ain't no mastermind, bro. You're just a corner boy with a laptop and better clothes. You stole from your mama same as me."
* **Counselor Mona (45):** Senior clinical therapist at Ward 4. Calm, immovable, immune to Adam's charm and bargaining. She enforces the structure with surgical neutrality.
