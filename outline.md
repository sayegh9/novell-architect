#### Ch 1: The Color of Dissonance
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** House of Bells Workshop, The Tiers
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Opening Image (0-4%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Sensory Overload → Frustrated Resignation
*   **Try-Fail Cycle:** No, and... (He fails to perfectly tune a bell, *and* his father’s criticism reinforces his sense of inadequacy.)
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cass is overwhelmed by the sonic chaos of the workshop, seeing it as a painful clash of jagged, colored light. He tries to focus on tuning a small bronze bell.
    2.  His father, Eddan, tests the bell. It’s a fraction of a cent sharp. Eddan’s voice is cold with disappointment. He lectures Cass on the necessity of purity and precision.
    3.  Cass notices Eddan’s left hand trembling, a symptom of Acoustic Tremor. Eddan hides it, his shame turning to anger. He orders Cass to clean the workshop.
    4.  While sweeping, Cass looks at a heavy, locked door at the back of the workshop, one he has been forbidden to open his entire life.
*   **Plants:** Eddan’s tremor; the locked room; Cass’s synesthesia as a painful weakness.
*   **Payoffs:** None.
*   **Character Movement:** Cass is established as an outsider in his own home, his unique perception a source of failure, not strength.
*   **The Lie:** The chapter demonstrates the Lie in action: Cass tries to master the orthodox system by tuning the bell perfectly, and his failure reinforces his belief that this is the only path, and he is failing at it.
*   **~Word Count:** 3,000

#### Ch 2: The Purity of the Crest
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** Lower Conservatorium grounds, The Crest
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Theme Stated (5-8%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Hopeful → Anxious
*   **Try-Fail Cycle:** Yes, but... (He gets the Tremor-Moss for his father, *but* the encounter with Elara reminds him of the vast gap between his abilities and the Aurally ideal.)
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cass makes his weekly trip to the Crest to buy a ration of Tremor-Moss for Eddan from a Guild dispensary. He hates the clean, sterile sound of the upper city.
    2.  He meets Elara, a student at the Conservatorium. She is practicing a complex harmonic, and Cass sees it as a perfect, shimmering braid of light. He feels a pang of envy.
    3.  Elara speaks passionately about her studies. She says, "The Prime Chord is perfect. It’s the impurities—in metal, in a voice, in a person—that cause collapse. Purity is the only thing that keeps the walls from falling down." This is the Theme Stated.
    4.  She mentions the vast, brittle archives of the Conservatorium, where the city’s entire sonic history is recorded on stone tablets.
*   **Plants:** Elara as a friend and believer in the system; the existence of the archives; the theme of purity vs. impurity.
*   **Payoffs:** None.
*   **Character Movement:** Cass’s goal is solidified: he wants to get into the Conservatorium like Elara, believing it’s the only way to gain power.
*   **The Lie:** Elara’s speech directly reinforces Cass’s Lie. She presents the system as a perfect meritocracy, making Cass believe his only option is to become "pure" enough to succeed within it.
*   **~Word Count:** 3,500

#### Ch 3: The Unpayable Debt
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** House of Bells Workshop
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Catalyst (9-12%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Numb Routine → Cold Fear
*   **Try-Fail Cycle:** No, and... (The family cannot pay the debt, *and* the threat is now immediate and absolute: seizure of their home.)
*   **Beats:**
    1.  A notary from the House of Corda arrives. It is not Maret herself, but a junior clerk. The air around him hums with the authority of his office.
    2.  He serves Eddan with a notice of final default on the loan taken out for Perin’s apprenticeship. The Guild stipend was supposed to cover it, but Perin’s contract voided the arrangement.
    3.  The terms are stark: pay the full amount in one month, or the House of Bells workshop and all its assets will be seized by the Corda.
    4.  After the notary leaves, Eddan is silent and frozen. Cass sees his father’s fear, not as sound, but as a colorless void. For the first time, Cass feels a surge of anger, not just helplessness.
*   **Plants:** The specifics of Perin's contract and the debt; the power of the Corda.
*   **Payoffs:** None.
*   **Character Movement:** The abstract problem of his brother's fate becomes a concrete, immediate crisis. He is forced out of his passive state.
*   **The Lie:** The system, in the form of the Corda, directly attacks his family. This is the first major crack in his belief that working within it can save them.
*   **~Word Count:** 3,000

#### Ch 4: The Sound of a Lie
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** The Tiers, a Basin market
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Debate (13-17%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Determined → Disgusted

*   **Plants:** The Dissonant Brand as a small-scale form of Tonal Law binding; the first perception of the "gap" or Null Chord.
*   **Payoffs:** None.
*   **Character Movement:** Cass learns that the established powers will not help him. He must look elsewhere.

#### Ch 5: A Father's Secret
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** House of Bells Workshop
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Debate (18-22%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Frustrated → Intrigued
*   **Try-Fail Cycle:** No, but... (He fails to convince his father to act, *but* he discovers a hidden object that offers a new path.)
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cass confronts Eddan, arguing that honor is meaningless if they lose their home. The argument escalates.
    2.  In his anger, Eddan slams his trembling hand on the workbench. The tremor is worse than Cass has ever seen. Eddan shouts, "You know nothing of ruin! Some lines must never be crossed!"
    3.  That night, unable to sleep, Cass is drawn to the sound of Eddan humming a strange, looping melody in his sleep—a lullaby Cass hasn't heard since childhood.
    4.  Following an intuition, Cass finds an old, dusty music box. He winds it, and it plays the same lullaby. He perceives the melody not as a solid color, but as something that seems to *unravel* the ambient sound around it. Inside the box, he finds a strangely shaped tuning fork, made of a dark, non-resonant metal.
*   **Plants:** The lullaby as the Null Chord; the special tuning fork.
*   **Payoffs:** None.
*   **Character Movement:** Cass moves from looking for external solutions to realizing there may be a secret, internal one.
*   **The Lie:** He discovers a tool and a sound that exist completely outside the orthodox system his Lie is built on.
*   **~Word Count:** 3,000

#### Ch 6: The First Unraveling
*   **POV:** Cassian
*   **Location:** House of Bells Workshop
*   **Save the Cat Beat:** Break into Two (23-25%)
*   **Emotional Arc:** Terrified → Resolute
*   **Try-Fail Cycle:** Yes, but... (He successfully unravels a minor binding, *but* the effort leaves him physically ill and terrified of his own power.)
*   **Beats:**
    1.  Cass takes the strange tuning fork and a piece of scrap metal bound to a block of wood with a simple Perfect Fifth. This is his new world.
    2.  He strikes the fork. It produces no audible sound, but Cass *sees* a perfect, sharp-edged shape of blackness.
    3.  He tries to hum the lullaby from the music box, matching the shape of the silence he sees. It feels unnatural, like inhaling instead of exhaling.
    4.  He focuses the silent hum on the bound metal. The golden light of the Perfect Fifth binding flickers, frays, and then dissolves into smoke. The metal clatters to the floor, unbound.
    5.  The backlash hits him—a wave of vertigo and nausea. He has done the impossible. He has broken a fundamental law. He knows this is his only path forward. He chooses to walk it.
*   **Plants:** The physical cost of using the Null Chord.
*   **Payoffs:** The "gap" from Ch 4 is identified and weaponized.
*   **Character Movement:** Cass makes the active choice to abandon the "proper" path and embrace the forbidden. He now has a plan.
*   **The Lie:** He actively rejects his Lie. He will not master the system; he will break its rules.
*   **~Word Count:** 3,500
