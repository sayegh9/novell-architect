# WORLD.MD: Cantamura

This document is the definitive reference for the world. It contains all established canon.

## Cosmology & History

The history of Cantamura is the history of its sound. Official records, kept by the Aurally on resonant stone tablets, tell a story of order imposed on chaos. Dissonant histories, whispered in the Basin, tell of a cage built from a song.

**Timeline of Major Events**

*   **The Age of Cacophony (Mythic Past):** Before Cantamura, the world was an unbound chaos of formless matter and raw sound. The founding myth states the **First Singers**, a group of seven ascetics, discovered the Great Amphitheater, a place of unique acoustic purity.
*   **The First Chord (c. 900 years ago):** The First Singers performed the **Prime Chord**, a perfect major chord that resonated with the stone of the amphitheater itself. This act of Tonal Law gave matter form, stability, and structure. It raised the city walls, smoothed the Tiers, and established the fundamental resonance that holds Cantamura together. This event is the basis for all law and religion. The Prime Chord must be maintained.
*   **The Schism of the Fifth (c. 450 years ago):** A doctrinal civil war erupted between two schools of Tonal Law. The **Orthodox** insisted on the purity of the perfect fifth as the primary interval for binding. The **Enharmonic** faction argued for the use of tempered intervals, believing them to be more efficient. The war was brutal, fought with sonic lances and shattering chords. The Orthodox won, casting the Enharmonics out and branding their teachings as heresy.
    *   **Present-day Tension:** This event codified Tonal Law into a rigid, unchangeable dogma. Experimentation is now taboo. The Aurally Council descends directly from the Orthodox school, and their primary fear is a return to that chaos. Whispers persist of Enharmonic texts hidden in the city's foundations.
*   **The Invention of the Corda (c. 300 years ago):** To rebuild the city and control the populace after the Schism, the Aurally developed the **Corda Contract**. Initially a tool for binding criminals to civic labor, it was quickly adopted by the burgeoning Guilds to secure a workforce. It transformed Cantamuran society from a caste system into one of explicit, magically-enforced servitude.
*   **The Great Tremor Plague (c. 40 years ago):** The Aurally Council commissioned the construction of the Conductor's Spire, the highest point in the city. The project required sustained, high-amplitude Tonal Law for over a decade. This caused a massive outbreak of **Acoustic Tremor** among the Resonator Guilds. Hundreds of masons and bellmakers, including the protagonist's grandfather, were lost to the degenerative disease.
    *   **Present-day Tension:** This event created a deep, simmering resentment between the Resonator Guilds and the Aurally Council. The Resonators see it as proof that the Aurally will sacrifice them for their own glory. The Aurally view it as a necessary, if tragic, cost of maintaining the city's integrity. It is the defining trauma of the protagonist's family.

## Magic System

### Hard Rules (Tonal Law)

Tonal Law is the physical manifestation of acoustic resonance. It is a form of sonic magic that allows practitioners to manipulate the physical world by producing precise frequencies and intervals. It is a science, not an art. Precision is paramount.

**Core Principles:**
*   **Resonant Frequency:** Every object and person has a unique resonant frequency. Identifying and manipulating this frequency is the foundation of all Tonal Law.
*   **Amplitude as Power:** The volume of a tone dictates its power. A louder tone has a greater physical effect.
*   **Purity of Pitch:** An impure or wavering pitch will cause the effect to fail, often with dangerous backlash, such as uncontrolled shattering or feedback.

**The Foundational Intervals:**
*   **The Prime Unity (Unison/Octave):** Reinforces the existing structure of an object. Used for maintenance and repair. Singing the Prime Unity into a cracking pillar reinforces its molecular bonds.
*   **The Perfect Fifth (The Bind):** The primary tool of construction. Creates a powerful, semi-permanent bond between two objects of the same material (stone to stone, steel to steel). The entire city is held together by layered Perfect Fifths.
*   **The Major Third (The Temper):** Alters the internal properties of a material. Can be used to harden steel for a chisel, increase the clarity of glass, or petrify wood. The effect is permanent.
*   **The Minor Second (The Sever):** A controlled dissonance. Creates a shearing force along a precise line. Used in quarries to cut stone and for demolition. Its use is heavily regulated by the Aurally; unauthorized use is a capital crime.

**Complex Harmonics:**
*   **The Corda Contract:** A binding spell placed on a person. It requires a complex chord progression (tonic, subdominant, dominant, tonic) sung precisely at the target's resonant frequency. The caster must focus the sound into a physical object (a chain link, a brass ring) which then becomes the anchor for the contract.
    *   **Effect:** The bound individual feels a constant, low-grade hum of the contract. Disobeying a direct order from the contract holder causes the hum to escalate into debilitating, nauseating pain. Attempting to flee the city causes seizures.
    *   **Cost to Caster:** The caster experiences a sympathetic backlash, permanently acquiring a minor tremor in their non-dominant hand for each contract they enact. Casting multiple contracts is a path to accelerated decay.

**COSTS AND LIMITATIONS:**
1.  **Acoustic Tremor:** The primary cost. All use of Tonal Law exposes the user to sonic energy that causes irreversible neurological damage. It begins as a slight tremor in the hands and progresses to full-body spasms, memory loss, and eventually, a catatonic state where the victim only hums a single, wavering note. Dissonant intervals and high amplitudes accelerate the disease. There is no cure, only mitigation through ingestion of Tremor-Moss.
2.  **Resonant Decay:** Materials manipulated by Tonal Law become "brittle" over time. The bonds must be reinforced with the Prime Unity every few decades, or they will collapse. Cantamura is in a constant state of sonic maintenance.
3.  **The Inverse Square Law:** The power of a tone diminishes sharply with distance. A Resonator must be within arm's reach of their work. This prevents large-scale, god-like manipulations. Building a wall requires a choir of masons, not a single powerful singer.

### Soft Magic (Cass's Gift)

Cassian, the protagonist, does not simply hear sound; he perceives it through a form of synesthesia.

*   **Perception:** He sees sound as color and texture. A pure tone is a clean, solid line of light. A chord is a woven braid of colors. Dissonance is a jagged, sickly green-black. The Prime Chord of the city is a constant, golden dome of light above him.
*   **The "Gaps":** His unique perception allows him to see the spaces *between* the notes—the negative space in the sonic spectrum. This is where he discovers the forbidden harmonic.
*   **The Null Chord:** This is not a sound, but a precise, structured *absence* of sound. It is a harmonic that perfectly cancels out the frequencies of the Prime Chord. When applied to a Tonal Law binding, it does not shatter it; it *unravels* it, causing the binding to revert to its unbound state as if it never existed.
*   **Cost:** The world is a constant sensory assault. Crowded rooms are a blinding, painful cacophony of light and noise, giving him severe migraines and nosebleeds. He must wear thick, insulated clothing and focus on simple sounds to stay grounded. Using his gift to perceive the "gaps" leaves him dizzy and disoriented for hours.

### Societal Implications

Tonal Law is not just magic; it is the organizing principle of Cantamuran society.

*   **Governance & Class:** The city is a rigid hierarchy based on vocal purity and pitch control.
    *   **The Aurally:** The ruling priest-academics who live on the Crest. They have perfect pitch and can produce the most complex harmonics. They legislate, judge, and maintain the Prime Chord.
    *   **The Resonators:** The middle class of skilled artisans (bellmakers, masons, smiths). They live on the Tiers. They have strong, stable voices and are trained in the Foundational Intervals. They build the city and bear the brunt of the Acoustic Tremor.
    *   **The Basin-Dwellers:** The lowest class. Their voices are considered impure, unsteady, or "tone-deaf." They perform manual labor that requires no Tonal Law. To be tone-deaf is to be legally disabled and barred from Guild membership.
*   **Economy:** The economy is based on resonant goods. Cantamuran bells are prized for their purity. Tuned steel from its forges holds the sharpest edge. The Resonator's Guild holds immense economic power, second only to the Aurally.
*   **Law and Order:** Justice is sonic. Minor crimes are punished with a **Dissonant Brand**, a painful, discordant tone sung into the skin that leaves a scar that hums faintly when the person lies. Major crimes result in a Corda Contract, binding the criminal to civic servitude.
*   **Family and Childhood:** Children are tested for pitch acuity at age five. A child with perfect pitch is a great honor and is sent to the Conservatorium to train with the Aurally. A child with a strong voice joins a Guild apprenticeship. A tone-deaf child is a source of shame. Cass, with his strange sensory abilities, was deemed to have an "unstable" pitch and was thus a disappointment.

## Geography

Cantamura is built into a massive, terraced limestone amphitheater carved from the Echoing Peaks. The city's structure is inseparable from its acoustic properties.

*   **The Crest:** The highest tier of the city, carved from white marble and resonant granite. Home to the Aurally Council, the Conservatorium, and the Conductor's Spire. The air is thin and clear.
    *   **Sensory Signature:** The sound is unnervingly clean; stray noises are absorbed by acoustic dampeners. The constant, pure hum of the Spire is felt in the teeth. The air smells of ozone and polished stone.
*   **The Tiers:** The broad, middle rings of the city where the Resonator Guilds live and work. A chaotic network of workshops, foundries, and family homes.
    *   **Sensory Signature:** A constant din of industry: the rhythmic clang of sonic hammers, the sharp ring of tuning forks, the drone of practice scales. The air is thick with the smell of hot metal, coal smoke, and sawdust. Rock dust coats everything.
*   **The Basin:** The lowest level of the city, at the bottom of the amphitheater. It is damp, crowded, and perpetually in shadow. Sound echoes strangely here, creating a muddy, disorienting sonic environment.
    *   **Sensory Signature:** A low, constant rumble from the city above. The smell of mildew, stagnant water, unwashed bodies, and cheap, greasy food. The air is heavy and wet.

**Neighboring Places:**

*   **The Echoing Peaks:** The jagged, wind-scoured mountains that form the amphitheater. They are rich in **Singing Stone**, the limestone that gives Cantamura its unique resonance. The peaks are treacherous, with unpredictable acoustic dead zones and resonating caves that can shatter a man's bones with a single misplaced shout.
*   **The Whispering Plains:** A vast grassland stretching out from the base of the mountains. The wind moving through the tall, silica-rich grasses creates a constant, low whisper that Cantamurans find deeply unsettling. It is home to nomadic tribes who use sign language and view sound as a dangerous, corrupting force.
*   **The Silent Forest:** A petrified forest a day's journey from the city. The crystalline trees absorb all sound. To step inside is to experience true, absolute silence, a sensation so alien to Cantamurans that it can drive them mad.

## Factions & Politics

Power in Cantamura is a struggle for control over the city's fundamental frequency.

*   **The Aurally Council:** The ruling body, led by High Conductor Valerius. They are arch-traditionalists, dedicated to preserving the Prime Chord and the stability it brings. They view any deviation from Orthodox Tonal Law as an existential threat. Their primary goal is maintaining the status quo at any cost.
*   **The Resonator's Guild:** A confederation of craft guilds, nominally led by Guildmaster Borin of the House of Hammers. They are the city's engine, but also its primary victims. They desire more political power, better access to Tremor-Moss, and restrictions on the use of the Corda Contract. They are pragmatic and deeply resentful of the Aurally's casual disregard for their lives.
*   **The Silenti:** A radical, secretive faction operating in the Basin. Composed of outcasts, tone-deaf laborers, and disillusioned Resonators. They believe Tonal Law is a prison and the Prime Chord is the lock. They actively seek the Null Chord, which they call the "Sound of Liberation," believing it will shatter the system of control and create true equality, regardless of the physical destruction it would cause.
*   **House of Bells:** The protagonist's family. A once-proud Guild family, now in decline. The father, Kael, is a master bellmaker but is deeply conservative and terrified of deviancy after losing his own father to the Tremor. The older brother, Lyren, was a prodigy whose ambition led him into a Corda contract he cannot escape. They represent the central conflict: torn between upholding the system and the desperate need to break it.

## Bestiary / Flora / Natural World

The natural world around Cantamura has adapted to or is defined by sonic properties.

*   **Glasswing Moths:** Insects whose crystalline wings vibrate at a perfect C-sharp when in flight. Their wings are harvested and used as resonant plates in delicate diagnostic instruments for tuning.
*   **Resonant Geodes:** Crystalline nodules found deep within the Echoing Peaks. When struck, they absorb the kinetic energy and release it as a single, impossibly pure tone that can last for weeks. They are used as master tuning references in the Conservatorium and are worth more than gold.
*   **Tremor-Moss:** A rare, phosphorescent lichen that grows only in acoustic dead zones within the mountains. It has sound-dampening properties. When brewed into a tea, it soothes the nervous system and slows the progression of the Acoustic Tremor. The Aurally control its harvest and distribution, creating a black market in the Basin.
*   **Gargoyles (Echo-bats):** Large, bat-like creatures that roost in the upper spires. They navigate not with sonar, but by mimicking the city's tones. An out-of-tune gargoyle screeching at night is considered a terrible omen.

## Cultural Details

Daily life is saturated with sonic ritual and custom.

*   **The Chord of Greeting:** Instead of waving or shaking hands, individuals hum their two-note family interval (e.g., a perfect fourth for the House of Bells, a major second for the House of Hammers). A friend responds by humming the same interval back. A superior expects you to hum their interval.
*   **Sonoculture:** Food and drink are often prepared using Tonal Law. "Tuned Wine" is aged in resonant casks to accelerate fermentation. "Vibrated Beef" is tenderized with low frequencies. The food on the Crest is known for its subtle, pure flavors, while Basin food is a dissonant clash of spices.
*   **The Tuning:** A coming-of-age ceremony at age sixteen. The youth's personal resonant frequency is measured and recorded in the city's registry. This day determines their future path—Conservatorium, Guild, or Basin. It is a day of immense celebration or profound grief.
*   **Public Dissonance:** Producing a deliberately unresolved, dissonant chord in public is the gravest of social taboos, equivalent to blasphemy and public indecency. It is seen as an attack on the very fabric of the city.
*   **The Day of Silence:** Once a year, on the anniversary of the signing of the Prime Chord, all non-essential sound ceases for 24 hours. The city holds its breath, listening to the pure hum of the foundations. It is a day of meditation and fear, as any flaw in the Prime Chord would be audible on this day.

## Internal Consistency Rules

These are hard physical and metaphysical laws of the world that cannot be violated.

1.  **Sound Requires a Medium:** Tonal Law cannot be produced in a vacuum or transmitted through the Silent Forest's trees. It is dependent on air, water, or solid matter.
2.  **Conservation of Energy:** Tonal Law does not create matter or energy from nothing. It reshapes and binds what is already there. The energy to do so comes from the caster's own body, channeled through their voice. This is why casters must be well-fed and strong.
3.  **No Action at a Distance:** All Tonal Law effects are strictly line-of-sight and subject to the inverse square law. You cannot bind a wall from across the city. You must touch the stone.
4.  **The Null Chord is an Unraveling, Not an Explosion:** The forbidden harmonic does not create a destructive force. It negates the Tonal Law that holds things together. Applying it to a pillar doesn't make the pillar explode; it makes it crumble into dust and gravel, as if the binding spell had never been cast. Applying it to the city's foundations would cause a catastrophic structural collapse.
5.  **Acoustic Tremor is Inevitable:** There is no way to use Tonal Law without exposure. The cost is absolute. Greater power means a faster decline. No one is immune.
